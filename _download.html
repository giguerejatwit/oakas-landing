Install OAKAS

One command. Everything you need — AI model, assistant, WhatsApp connection — installed on your machine in minutes.

Mac

Download and double-click to install. That's it.

Download OAKAS Installer

1

Double-click the downloaded file

Terminal opens automatically and installs everything — AI model, assistant, all dependencies. Takes 3-5 minutes.

2

Start OAKAS

Run openclaw gateway start in your terminal.

3

Scan the QR code

Open WhatsApp on your phone, go to Linked Devices, and scan the QR code. That's it — start texting your AI.
