Common Questions

Yeah but...

Do I need to be techy?
Not even a little. If you can text, you can use OAKAS. All you need is WhatsApp. No dashboards to learn, no extra software to install. You literally just text it what you need: "send the invoice," "book the estimate," "what's my schedule." It handles it.
What if it says something wrong to my customer?
During the first 14 days, we tune OAKAS to your voice, your business, and your rules. You set the boundaries: what it can say, what it should check with you first, how it talks to people. It sounds like you, not a robot. And you can always override it.
Is this going to spam my customers?
No. OAKAS only reaches out when you tell it to, or when a lead contacts you first. It doesn't mass-blast anyone. Follow-ups are personalized and timed, like a good assistant would do, not like a marketing robot.
Why not just hire a virtual assistant?
A decent VA costs $500-1,500/mo and works business hours. OAKAS starts at $50/mo and works 24/7. Nights, weekends, holidays. It never calls in sick, never forgets a follow-up, and responds to leads in seconds instead of hours. Plus there's nothing to manage. No training, no scheduling, no HR.
Can I actually cancel my other apps?
That's the goal. With OakasNode ($199/mo), most solo operators can cancel their CRM, scheduling tool, answering service, and invoicing software within the first month. Even OakasOne ($50/mo) can replace your calendar app, email client, and scheduling tool. We'll walk you through exactly which ones to drop during setup. Some clients keep QuickBooks for tax purposes, and that's fine — OAKAS integrates with it on our Team plan.
What happens if I cancel?
Nothing weird. No contracts, no cancellation fee. You stop paying, the service stops. We'll export your data if you want it. We'd rather earn your business every month than lock you into something you don't want.
How is this so affordable?
We're a small operation and we plan to stay that way. No bloated sales team, no VC money to burn, no enterprise features you'll never use. OakasOne starts at $50/mo because a personal assistant shouldn't cost a fortune. OakasNode at $199/mo replaces $600+ in software. The price will go up eventually.
