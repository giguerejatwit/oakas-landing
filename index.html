The Cost of Doing It by Hand

Every week, your team loses hours to work that software should be doing.

It never shows up on an invoice. It hides inside the week: the CRM updated by hand, the lead answered an hour late, the same report rebuilt every Monday, the data copied from one system to another and back again.

All of it is recurring. And it quietly drains the one resource you can’t buy more of: the attention of the people who actually run your company.

The Process

Three phases, then it’s yours.

Phase 01

Align

A short, close read of where the hours go. We map the manual work, agree on the outcome that matters, and scope the smallest system that moves the needle. No boil-the-ocean roadmaps.

Phase 02

Build

We design and ship custom software against your real workflow, not a template. You see working software early and often, refined against the way your team actually operates.

Phase 03

Hand-Off

It goes live inside your business, your team is trained, and you own everything: the code, the data, the accounts. No lock-in. And if you’d rather we keep hosting and maintaining it for you, we can, for a simple monthly fee. Your call.

You keep everything. Source code, data and keys are handed over in full, engineered and documented to run without us. Every build is fixed-scope and priced before work starts. No retainers.
