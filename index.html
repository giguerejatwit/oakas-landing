Common Questions

Yeah but...

Do I need to be techy?
Not even a little. If you can text, you can use OAKAS. There's no app to download, no dashboard to learn, no software to install. You literally just text it what you need — "send the invoice," "book the estimate," "what's my schedule" — and it handles it.
What if it says something wrong to my customer?
During the first 14 days, we tune OAKAS to your voice, your business, and your rules. You set the boundaries — what it can say, what it should check with you first, how it talks to people. It sounds like you, not a robot. And you can always override it.
Is this going to spam my customers?
No. OAKAS only reaches out when you tell it to, or when a lead contacts you first. It doesn't mass-blast anyone. Follow-ups are personalized and timed — like a good assistant would do, not like a marketing robot.
Why not just hire a virtual assistant?
A decent VA costs $500-1,500/mo and works business hours. OAKAS is $199/mo and works 24/7 — nights, weekends, holidays. It never calls in sick, never forgets a follow-up, and responds to leads in seconds instead of hours. Plus there's nothing to manage — no training, no scheduling, no HR.
Can I actually cancel my other apps?
That's the goal. Most solo operators can cancel their CRM, scheduling tool, answering service, and invoicing software within the first month. We'll walk you through exactly which ones to drop during setup. Some clients keep QuickBooks for tax purposes — that's fine, OAKAS works alongside it too.
What happens if I cancel?
Nothing weird. No contracts, no cancellation fee. You stop paying, the service stops. We'll export your data if you want it. We'd rather earn your business every month than lock you into something you don't want.
How is this only $199/mo?
We're a small operation and we plan to stay that way. No bloated sales team, no VC money to burn, no enterprise features you'll never use. We built OAKAS for one-person shops because that's who we care about. The price will go up eventually — founding members keep their rate forever.
