Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 16, 2026

OAKAS ("we", "us", "our") operates the oakas.ai website and the OAKAS AI business assistant service. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

  • Account information — name, email, phone number, and business details you provide during signup.
  • Messages — text messages you send to and receive from your OAKAS assistant via WhatsApp.
  • Business data — contacts, invoices, jobs, appointments, and expenses you create through the service.
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe. We do not store your credit card details.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the OAKAS assistant service.
  • To process your requests — generating invoices, scheduling appointments, managing contacts, etc.
  • To send you service-related messages via WhatsApp.
  • To process payments through Stripe.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. Messages and business data are encrypted in transit. We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

Third-Party Services

  • Stripe — payment processing (stripe.com/privacy)
  • Meta / WhatsApp Business API — messaging (whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy)
  • AI providers — to generate responses. Your messages may be processed by third-party AI models. We do not use your data to train AI models.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with the third-party services listed above as necessary to operate the service.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at jake@oakas.ai for any requests.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via WhatsApp or email.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email jake@oakas.ai
